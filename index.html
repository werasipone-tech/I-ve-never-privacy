Privacy Policy for “Never Have I Ever (18+)”

App Owner: Klevin Vladimir
Privacy Contact: werasipone@gmail.com
Effective Date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information while you play, use the free trial, and purchase the full version.

Game Data

The app does not require users to create a separate account. Players may use fictional names, nicknames, or pseudonyms.

The app stores the selected language and the history of up to ten most recent games locally on the device. Game history may include player names or nicknames, avatars, scores, the winner, the date of the game, language, game mode, game pace, played questions, and players’ answers from completed rounds. When new games are saved, older records exceeding this limit are removed.

This information is used only to provide game functionality and display previous results. Some answers may relate to personal or intimate topics. Such information is not transmitted to the app owner’s server, is not published, and is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

People who have access to your device and the app may be able to view saved game history.

Purchases and Free Trial

Purchases of the full version and activation of the free trial are processed through Apple App Store services.

The app receives information necessary to determine purchase status, access eligibility, trial status, and purchase restoration.

The app does not receive your bank card number or other payment details. Apple Account and payment information are processed by Apple in accordance with Apple’s Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ .

Analytics, Advertising, and Permissions

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

The app does not track your activity across other apps or websites, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not sell personal or game-related data.

The app does not require access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Playing game sounds does not involve recording audio.

Apple system features, including diagnostics and backups, are governed by your device settings and Apple’s terms. The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization for game history. Local app data may be included in system backups depending on your device settings.

Data Storage and Deletion

You may delete individual games or clear game history using the controls available in the app’s history section.

The selected language remains stored until you change the setting or remove the app’s data.

Deleting the app from your device removes its local app data. Using Apple’s offload feature may preserve app data on the device.

Local data may also remain in previously created device backups and may be restored together with those backups. Backup management is handled through Apple services or through the backup method selected by the user.

Deleting game history or deleting the app does not remove purchase records maintained by Apple and does not restart the free trial.

The app owner does not maintain a server-side copy of your game history and therefore cannot access, restore, or remotely delete game history from your device.

Contact

For questions regarding privacy or data handling, please contact: werasipone@gmail.com.

If you send an email, the app owner may receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any materials you choose to attach.

Please do not include intimate game answers or other information that is not necessary to resolve your request.

Gmail, provided by Google, is used to receive and process email inquiries.

Information received through email inquiries is used only to respond to and resolve the request and is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Age Audience

The app contains content intended for an adult audience and is not intended for children. Please refer to the age rating displayed on the app’s App Store page.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality or data handling practices change. The current effective date is displayed at the top of this document.

Material changes to the way data is handled will be reflected in the updated Privacy Policy and, where appropriate, communicated to users within the app.
